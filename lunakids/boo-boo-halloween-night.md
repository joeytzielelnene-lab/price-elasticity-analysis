# LunaKids — "Boo-Boo's Halloween Night"

**Format:** One continuous nursery-rhyme song with a story · **Length:** 4:00 · **Audience:** ages 2–5
**Tempo:** 100 BPM, 4/4 → 100 bars total (1 bar = 2.4 s, 8 bars = 19.2 s)
**Key:** C major → modulates up to D major at 3:31 for the final chorus
**Instruments:** xylophone (melody), glockenspiel (sparkles & hooks), plus soft upright bass, light shaker, and finger snaps on the chorus
**Vocals:** warm, smiley female lead + a small kids' chorus on every call-and-response ("Boo!", "Glow!", "Squeak!", "Wheee!", the counting)
**Look:** 3D cartoon, round and squishy characters, purple/orange/starry-night palette. Nothing scary: no sharp teeth, no skulls, no jump scares, no dark corners. Everything glows warm.

## 2. Lyrics

### Intro (0:00)
*(glockenspiel sparkle run, soft xylophone)*
**Narrator (spoken, whispery-happy):** "It's Halloween night in Moonbeam Hollow... ooh, look! It's Boo-Boo!"

### Verse 1 (0:10)
On Halloween the moon was bright,
Little Boo-Boo floated out tonight.
He chased a leaf that twirled around,
Up and down and round and round!
Past the trees and past the hill,
Everything was quiet and still...
He looked to the left, he looked to the right —
"Oh my! I'm lost on Halloween night!"

Which way, which way, should we go?
**Boo!** *(Boo!)* **Boo!** *(Boo!)* Sing with me —

### Verse 2: Pip the Pumpkin (0:48)
Then down the path, what did he see?
A round orange pumpkin, smiling with glee!

I'll be your friend, and that makes me glad!"
Pip took a breath and — oh, so bright! —
He lit the path with a warm, warm light.
Glow, glow, glowing near,
"Come along, Boo-Boo, the path is clear!"

"Hi, I'm Bella! Can I join in?"

**Style prefix** (put this at the start of every prompt):
> Premium 3D animated children's cartoon, soft rounded squishy character design, smooth clay-like materials, gentle subsurface glow, cozy Halloween night, palette of deep purple, warm orange and twinkling starry sky, big friendly moon, soft volumetric light, bright and cheerful (not dark), shallow depth of field, smooth gentle camera motion, 16:9, high detail.

**Negative prompt** (use on every scene):
> scary, creepy, horror, sharp teeth, fangs, skulls, skeletons, blood, spiders, angry faces, crying, dark shadows, jump scare, realistic humans, distorted faces, extra limbs, text, watermark, logo
