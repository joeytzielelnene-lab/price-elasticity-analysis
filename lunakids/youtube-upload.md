## Steps to publish (5 minutes)
1. Go to **studio.youtube.com** → **Create (+)** → **Upload videos**.
2. Drag in `LunaKids_BooBoo_Halloween_Night.mp4`.
3. Paste the title, description and tags from above.
4. Upload the thumbnail `LunaKids_BooBoo_thumbnail.jpg`.
5. Audience → **Made for kids**.
6. Visibility → **Unlisted** → watch it once → change to **Public**.
7. Copy the link (`https://youtu.be/...`) to share it.

> ⚠️ **Watch out for scams:** after publishing, you may get emails or messages offering "guaranteed views," "monetization in 24 hours" or "copyright problems — click here." YouTube only contacts you through **YouTube Studio**. Never give out your password and never pay for views.
